Cystic Fibrosis: Airway Pathogens and Culture Media

Study classic age-related colonization patterns and high-yield culture clues. The curves are illustrative, not current epidemiologic estimates.

Recognize the bacteria · Gram stain and morphology

Pseudomonas aeruginosa

Gram-negative rod · Pink · Oxidase-positive · MacConkey: non-lactose fermenter

Staphylococcus aureus

Gram-positive cocci in clusters · Purple · Mannitol salt agar

Haemophilus influenzae

Gram-negative coccobacillus · Pink · Chocolate agar · Factors X and V

Burkholderia cepacia complex

Gram-negative rods · Pink · Selective B. cepacia agar

Clinical reminder: multiple organisms can coexist, and Pseudomonas aeruginosa can be acquired in childhood.
