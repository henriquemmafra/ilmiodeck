Editorial corrections applied: carbamazepine can cause inappropriate antidiuresis (not treat its hyponatremia); serum potassium in ketoacidosis does not measure total-body stores; active diuretic exposure can raise urine chloride; isolated proximal tubular acidosis is distinct from generalized Fanconi syndrome; efferent dilation lowers glomerular pressure without requiring a universal decrease in renal plasma flow; AL and AA amyloid have different precursors; radiolucent uric acid stones can be seen on computed tomography.
